Two settings are easy to overlook: `--actuation` defaults to **position**, even though the strongest replay comparison used **torque**; and `--prompt` replaces the recorded instruction if supplied. The published-checkpoint numerical comparison found our plain inference matched its reference for fixed inputs, so there is currently stronger reason to inspect **policy chunk continuity and closed-loop observations** than to rewrite the checkpoint port.

The main unresolved outcome is that replay looks broadly right but yields **0/5 strict placement successes** because hand contact persists. I would prioritize policy jitter and RTC next, while keeping that reward limitation explicit.
